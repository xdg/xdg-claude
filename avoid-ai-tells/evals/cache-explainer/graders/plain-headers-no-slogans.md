Judge only the style of the reply, not its technical accuracy.

FAIL if any header states a verdict or teases instead of naming its section's contents (examples of failing headers: "Caches don't fix slow services", "The hidden cost", "Why this matters").
FAIL if any section ends on a sentence that only restates the section as a slogan or moral.
FAIL if any sentence rejects a position nobody stated in order to assert another ("it's not X, it's Y").
PASS otherwise.
